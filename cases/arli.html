Arli, 2025 to 2026

150 conversations, one decision: we closed Arli

How two founders built an AI cost-simulation platform for construction, tested it with the market, and stopped before scaling.

The idea

Arli was an AI-based platform to simulate construction costs. I co-founded it in August 2025 with one partner, and I led product.

What we built

We took it from concept to production as a two-person team. Both of us worked across the stack; I led the frontend, the Cloudflare and SSL setup, and the LLM integration through OpenRouter. Arli was selected for Unicorn Factory Lisboa's Contech acceleration programme.

Testing the market

We talked to more than 150 construction companies to understand their needs and how much they would pay, and fed what we heard into product and go-to-market decisions. The business model we designed combined a recurring SaaS subscription with transaction fees.

The decision

The validation showed that companies weren't willing to pay enough to build a sustainable business. We closed Arli before scaling it, rather than spending another year and more money on a product the market had already priced too low.

We built first and validated second. Next time I'll do it the other way round.

What I took from it

Test whether people will pay before writing the product. Talking to customers is cheap; a year of building is not.
