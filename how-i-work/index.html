How I work

A short guide for anyone thinking of working with me: how I make decisions and what to expect from me.

  1. I think out loud before I commit

    I like to stress-test a decision by talking through the trade-offs with someone. Once it's made, I move fast.

  2. I'll tell you when I disagree

    And I'd like the same from you. Direct pushback saves everyone time, especially when a plan rests on a wrong assumption.

  3. I check the numbers

    Accuracy matters to me. If a figure is going in front of a client, an investor or a professor, I want to know where it came from.

  4. I cut until it's short

    My first drafts are long. I keep editing until every line does a job, whether it's an email, a pitch or a slide.

  5. I validate before I build

    That's the lesson from closing Arli. I now test whether people will pay before anyone writes the product.

  6. I use AI as a working tool

    I use LLMs, connectors and automations every day, to build small tools and to get through research and analysis faster. My engineering background helps me know where they break.

Why an engineer is moving into business

AI is making technical execution cheaper every year. I think the edge over the next decade will come from judgement, commercial ownership and leading people. That's what I'm building with the master's at Nova SBE, while keeping the technical depth to talk to engineers and know what's possible.
